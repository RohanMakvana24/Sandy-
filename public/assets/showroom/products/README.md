# Sandy collection packshots

Eight individual product photographs were generated with the built-in image_gen tool and transparent_background: true. Full-resolution PNGs preserve the original alpha (1024 x 1536 portraits; jars 1536 x 1024). Matching 768px WebP previews preserve alpha and reduce the eight initial image downloads to approximately 375 KiB combined. Product close-up uses the original PNG rather than scaling the small preview.

The final prompt set is scripts/showroom-product-prompts.json. Images are original AI-created Sandy packaging presentations, not verified stock photography or proof of salon inventory. Names, sizes printed in the generated imagery, actual brands, prices and stock require salon approval.

Basket selection is available for all collection entries, independently of purchase availability. Unknown prices remain Price on request; no invented price or checkout is shown. Selections persist for the current browser session. A configured verified VITE_SALON_WHATSAPP enables a product inquiry containing names and quantities.

Every displayed shelf packshot maps to a catalogue entry and is selectable. Shelf products use actual volumetric bottle, jar, tube, pump and dropper geometry, shared materials, curved high-resolution labels and contact shadows. These meshes respond to room lighting and preserve depth from side views. The AI packshots are used in product cards, the basket and image close-ups. Drag rotates the camera; wheel and +/- controls adjust bounded camera zoom; product close-up has 100-300% zoom and scrolling.
